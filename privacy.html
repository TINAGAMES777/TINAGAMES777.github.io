Privacy Policy

Glass & Chill

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Tina Games Studio operates Glass & Chill, a mobile water sort puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and shared when you use the game.

Publication review: The data practices described below must be checked against the final released version of Glass & Chill, including its SDK settings, before this policy is used for the public launch.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled, information processed by the game or its service providers may include:

  • Device identifiers and advertising identifiers.
  • Gameplay progress, game settings and player identifiers.
  • Purchase information and entitlement status.
  • Technical information, including app version, device information and diagnostic data.

The precise data categories depend on the final implementation and the settings of the services used.

2. Advertising – Google AdMob

Glass & Chill uses or plans to use Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process device information, advertising identifiers and information about ad interactions, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Game Services – PlayFab

Glass & Chill uses or plans to use Microsoft PlayFab for game-related services, which may include player identifiers, game progress and optional player profiles.

See Microsoft's Privacy Statement.

4. In-App Purchases – RevenueCat

Glass & Chill plans to offer an optional Remove Ads purchase. RevenueCat may be used to manage purchase entitlements and subscription or purchase-related information.

Payments are processed through the applicable app store. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

5. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to operate the game, save progress, manage purchases, deliver advertisements, maintain security, diagnose technical issues and improve the user experience.

6. Sharing and International Processing

Information may be processed by service providers supporting advertising, game functionality and purchases. These providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence, subject to applicable legal requirements.

7. Data Retention and Your Rights

Information is retained only as appropriate for the relevant purposes and applicable legal obligations. Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, or to object to certain processing.

For privacy requests, contact the email address below.

8. Children's Privacy

The game is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Age-related restrictions, advertising settings and any additional parental consent requirements must be applied according to the game's final target audience.

9. Security

We aim to use reasonable safeguards to protect information processed through the game and its service providers. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the game, its services or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page.
